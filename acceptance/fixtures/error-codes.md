# Troubleshooting

## Faults

A fault means something broke. Faults and failures are logged automatically.

## Crashes

If the app crashes, the failure is reported and a bug is filed for the team.

## E-4012

E-4012: the payment gateway timed out. Retry the payment in a minute.

## Office hours

Support staff answer from 9 to 5 on weekdays at the office.
