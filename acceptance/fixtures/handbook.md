# Employee handbook

Welcome to the company. This handbook explains how we work and what you can expect from us.

## Leave

Everyone gets 25 days of paid leave per year. Unused leave carries over for three months.
Sick leave does not count towards your annual leave.

## Refunds

The refund window is 30 days. Customers may return any product within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.
After 30 days we offer store credit instead. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

## Security

Never share your password. Lock your screen when you leave your desk.
Report suspicious emails to the security team straight away.
